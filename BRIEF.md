# Work sample brief (mock, written for practice)

**Time allowed:** about 60 minutes (up to 120). Return a working prototype and a brief explanation of your approach.

## Problem

A large retail client hires entry-level contact-centre agents at scale, about 40,000 applicants a year. Its current screen is a multiple-choice test, which hiring managers say does not tell them whether a candidate can actually *write* to an upset customer.

The client wants a short **written-response task**. The candidate reads a customer complaint and writes a reply of about 80 to 200 words. An AI system then scores the reply on **customer-focused written communication**, and recruiters use that score to decide who goes forward to interview.

## What we ask you to do

1. Build a working prototype of the task and the AI scoring. A candidate (or reviewer) should be able to enter a reply and see a score with a justification a recruiter could understand.
2. Show how you would know whether the scores can be trusted.
3. Write a brief explanation (no more than two pages) of your approach, key decisions, risks, and what it would take to use this in live hiring in the US and EU.

## Notes

- You may use AI coding tools and any LLM API. Do not use real candidate data.
- We care more about your judgement than about polish.
- The reviewers will include psychometricians, a product manager and an engineer.
